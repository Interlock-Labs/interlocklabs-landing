05

Voice

The page speaks as one person: plain, professional, declarative. Interlock is an evidence ledger with many uses; it never routes, escalates, or certifies, and it never decides for the customer.

ALWAYS
  • Evidence ledger, decision record, workflow
  • Verbs: check, flag, measure, record, keep
  • Record states: WITHIN RULES / NEEDS A PERSON
  • "You decide who decides; the ledger records it."
  • Insurance vocabulary only in the liability section and footer
  • Professional register a risk officer can forward
NEVER
  • PASS / FAIL badges or verdict stamps
  • Route, escalate, certify, guarantee, seal
  • Insurance language above the fold
  • Pricing or "free" on the public page
  • Insider doctrine lines as page copy
  • Warm-tinted neutrals, decorative color, gradients
